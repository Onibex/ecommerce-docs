# Why can't I find some materials?

> **Source:** English version of the Spanish FAQ article “¿Por qué no encuentro algunos materiales?” in the Onibex Help Center — <https://help.onibex.com/portal/en/kb/articles/por-qu%C3%A9-no-encuentro-algunos-materiales>.

Not all existing materials or products are available to all users; there are multiple conditions that can prevent the end user from finding a given material, such as:

1. Credit limit
2. Price difference
3. Channels in which the user has been registered
4. Internal customer policies
5. The material / product was deactivated

If you have a case where the customer does not see a material that they should be seeing, please report it to your internal IT team and share the customer number or name, along with the materials that need to be displayed.
