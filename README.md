> **The B2B/B2C eCommerce platform built for SAP.** Fully integrated with your SAP ERP to manage inventory, pricing, orders, and customer engagement in real time. From a Proof of Concept to a live storefront in as little as 10 days.

This repository is the official documentation hub for **Onibex eCommerce 360°**, the SAP-integrated self-service commerce platform that empowers wholesale and retail businesses to sell online without disconnecting from their SAP core.

## What is eCommerce 360°?

**eCommerce 360°** is an all-inclusive B2B and B2C commerce platform designed specifically for enterprises running SAP ERP. Unlike generic eCommerce platforms that require complex middleware to connect to SAP, eCommerce 360° is natively integrated. Every product, price, inventory level, and order flows in real time between your storefront and your SAP system, with no batch jobs, no data drift, and no reconciliation nightmares.

Built with business owners, product engineers, sales teams, and end customers in mind, eCommerce 360° gives your organization a modern, self-service commerce experience while preserving the SAP-verified processes your business already runs on.

## Key Capabilities

### 🛒 Self-Service Commerce Platform

Give your customers a modern, intuitive shopping experience with everything they need to do business with you 24/7:

- **Product catalog** with rich media, categorization, and search.
- **Real-time inventory** by plant and warehouse.
- **Customer-specific pricing** driven directly from SAP conditions.
- **Order placement** with SAP-native validation.
- **Order history and tracking** with live status updates.
- **Account statements** and self-service AR visibility.

### 🔗 Native SAP Integration

No middleware. No batch delays. eCommerce 360° talks directly to SAP:

- **Real-time order flow** into SAP Sales Orders.
- **Live inventory** from SAP MM (`MARD`, `MSEG`).
- **Customer-specific pricing** from SAP SD pricing conditions.
- **Delivery and billing** synchronized with SAP.
- **Master data governance** with SAP as the source of truth.

### 💬 Communication and Collaboration Tools

Turn your storefront into a collaboration hub between B2B Buyers, Sales Reps, and Customer Service:

- **Push notifications** for order updates, back-in-stock alerts, and campaigns.
- **Integrated ticketing system** for support requests.
- **Live chat** between customers and sales/support agents.
- **Reports and analytics** on customer behavior, sales performance, and support metrics.

### 🎓 Onboarding and Self-Training

Reduce time-to-value for your customers and internal teams:

- **Virtual tour** for first-time users.
- **Self-explanatory UI** built on years of user research across industries.
- **Self-service help center** with searchable documentation.

### ☁️ Enterprise-Grade Infrastructure

- **AWS-certified** infrastructure for enterprise security, reliability, and scalability.

## Why eCommerce 360° vs. Generic eCommerce Platforms

Generic eCommerce platforms treat SAP as an afterthought. eCommerce 360° is built around SAP from day one. Here's what that means in practice:

| Common Limitation of Generic Platforms | How eCommerce 360° Solves It |
|---|---|
| SAP data synced via batch jobs, causing pricing and inventory drift. | Real-time bidirectional integration with SAP. |
| Customer-specific pricing requires custom middleware. | Native support for SAP pricing conditions out of the box. |
| Order errors due to SAP validation failures after checkout. | Pre-checkout validation against SAP business rules. |
| No visibility of the customer's actual SAP account state. | Live account statements, orders, and deliveries in-app. |
| Long implementation timelines (6-12 months). | Proof of Concept integrated with SAP in **10 days**. |
